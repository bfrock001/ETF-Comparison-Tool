7. Metrics & Methodology Limitations

  • Returns are computed from dividend-adjusted closing prices; they assume reinvestment of distributions and ignore taxes, transaction costs, bid-ask spreads, and fund loads or redemption fees.
  • When funds have different inception or data-start dates, comparisons are aligned to the latest common start date among the selected funds; a fund's full history may therefore not be reflected in a given comparison.
  • CAGR, maximum drawdown, and annualized volatility are standard but simplified statistics computed on the resolved period only; they are sensitive to the exact start and end dates you choose.
  • The Tool does not account for fund expense ratios beyond what is embedded in adjusted prices, tracking error, liquidity, tax efficiency, or any qualitative factor that matters to a real investment decision.
