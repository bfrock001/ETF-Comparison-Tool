AUM = fund net assets. Fees = net expense ratio. YTD is cumulative; 1Y is total return; 3Y / 5Y / 10Y / Life are annualized. A blank return cell means the fund is younger than that window. Sharpe is a risk-adjusted score — annualized return over volatility for the trailing 5 years (ending on the data date above), at a 4% risk-free rate; higher is better. Funds younger than 5 years use their full history since inception, so their Sharpe reflects a shorter, not-strictly-comparable window.
